This template is provided for website setup and should be reviewed for your actual business, jurisdiction, analytics configuration and advertising setup before publication.

Diagnostic data

Connection Health runs diagnostic logic in your browser. Requests used for reachability and speed testing are sent directly from your browser to the configured third-party endpoints. Those providers may receive standard request information such as your IP address and browser metadata under their own policies.

Local storage

The app may use browser localStorage to retain diagnostic history and settings on your device. This data is not automatically uploaded to a Connection Health application server in the default static deployment.

Advertising

If Google AdSense is enabled, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertising. Google and its partners may use advertising cookies based on visits to this site and/or other sites. Users can control personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Your final policy should include every disclosure required by your AdSense account and applicable law.
