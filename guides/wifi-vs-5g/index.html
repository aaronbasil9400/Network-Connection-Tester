Wi-Fi vs 5G

Compare Wi-Fi and cellular connections for everyday performance.

Wi-Fi and 5G can both deliver high throughput, but their performance characteristics differ. Wi-Fi depends heavily on your access point and local interference; cellular depends on radio conditions, network load and operator routing.

When Wi-Fi wins

A well-positioned modern access point with a fast fixed connection often provides stable indoor performance.

When cellular wins

Strong cellular coverage can outperform congested or poorly placed Wi-Fi, especially when the fixed connection is slow.
